# ideas

Junk drawer. No rules, no naming convention, no cleanup.

Drop half-patterns, four-bar loops, a chord voicing you liked, a line you want to
try in Spanish. Nothing in here has to work, be finished, or be findable.

The only thing that matters: when something in here turns out to be good, move it
to `../tracks/` and give it a `notes.md`. That is the promotion. Until then it
stays here and nobody has to justify it.

Do not tidy this folder. A junk drawer you keep organised is a junk drawer you
stop using, and then the half-ideas have nowhere to go and they stop getting
written down at all.
