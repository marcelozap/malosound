About this sketch

A local beat sketchpad, not a replacement for your recording software. Drum and bass sounds are synthesized in your browser, not generated by AI. WAV exports contain the beat; recorded takes contain your microphone and the beat together. Browser input latency can affect vocal timing. There is no automatic pitch correction or alignment.

Your pattern and lyric scratchpad are saved in this browser when storage is available. Recordings are not saved automatically. Microphone access starts only when you press Record and ends when you stop the take. Recording requires localhost or HTTPS. No libraries, accounts, subscriptions, or external audio services.
