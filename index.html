VERIFIED ARTIFACT

Victoria is the first proof package.

  • Audio: vicotira9-08pm.mp3
  • Analysis: AudioAnalysisV1 with beat times, RMS, zero-crossing, spectral rolloff, and provenance.
  • Ledger: Ed25519/SHA-256 hash-chain receipt with verified chain integrity.
  • Boundary: no smart contract deployed, no token minted, no wallet connected.
ARTIST CASE

Why it matters.

  • Artists can publish a verifiable release without asking a streaming platform to be the source of truth.
  • Supporters can keep a local file and a receipt that proves exactly what they downloaded.
  • Royalty metadata can be written down before any chain, marketplace, or distributor is involved.
  • The community gets a guide: how the release was made, what the proof means, and where the proof stops.
