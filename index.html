MaloSound is an audiovisual project by Marcelo Zapata. Fixed rules turn recorded market prices into musical notes and color. Velocity and acceleration shape the orbit.

Eight pitch landmarks, from low to high. Boundaries fall halfway between landmarks; an exact tie takes the higher note.

Range
The trailing five trading sessions, including this day. The lowest and highest observed five-minute closes are frozen as anchors. The open is not an anchor.
Price
clamp((smoothed close - low) / (high - low), 0, 1)
The nearest landmark sets the note. A flat range gives A and a stationary orbit.
Smoothing
A centered, 11-observation cubic Savitzky-Golay fit. One-minute spacing for the session; five-minute spacing for the context voice.
Motion
The fitted price's first derivative is velocity (price/minute); its second is acceleration (price/minute squared). Each axis is scaled to this session's own maximum absolute value. Similar shapes need not mean equal-sized moves.
Pitch
A minor: semitone offsets 0, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12. The session starts at A3 (220 Hz); context is an octave below, A2 (110 Hz). frequency = base × 2^(semitone / 12)
Color
Hue is semitone × 30°, modulo 360°. OKLCH lightness 0.80 and chroma 0.16 stay fixed. Both A octaves share a color. Color means pitch, not profit.
Sound
Notes plays the two price voices. House is their pre-rendered arrangement with a 120 BPM beat. Both use one clock: a market minute takes 0.5 seconds; the session lasts 3:15.
Gaps
Five observations at each end of every run are omitted. Missing data splits the path; no gap or overnight interval is connected. Derived voices and the marker stop where their observations end; House drums may continue.
Playback
The price readout is the recorded, unsmoothed one-minute close available in the instrument document. Empty readouts identify missing data or smoothing edges. Reduced motion steps between recorded-minute positions.
Perspective
This is retrospective, not a live quote or signal. Centered smoothing uses later observations, and the anchors include the completed day. The faint path previews the whole recorded session.
