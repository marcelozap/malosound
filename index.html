Reading the journal

Observations describe the market data shown. Reflections and music are my interpretations, not predictions.

Simulated examples are illustrations, not actual trades. A market chart alone does not document my trades or returns; only explicitly documented results should be read as trading results.

Gold line segments mark winning trades; blue marks losing trades, between documented entry and exit times. Neutral gray segments carry no outcome overlay. These colors do not assess execution quality; my execution reviews remain separate.
