# MaloSound: market melody

Built-in image generation. Marcelo requested stock movement becoming sung
phrases: do-re-mi rising, mi-re-do falling. No guitar. Decorative artwork only;
not observed price data, an actual trade record, or a generated audio recording.
Earlier artwork retained. Local homepage, cover CSS and social image metadata
now reference assets/brand/market-melody-v5.png. Not deployed to production or
uploaded to LinkedIn.

Prompt:

Create a finished wide landscape MaloSound brand artwork, approximately 1.9:1, for a personal options trader's market journal and its LinkedIn thumbnail. Concept: market movement becoming a SUNG MELODY, not an instrument brand. Deep ink-black backdrop, restrained gold and petrol blue. Across the middle and lower half, a clear thin angular GOLD STOCK PRICE LINE rises and falls, with a few small elegant gold candlesticks toward the left. Moving right the same visual gesture loosens into delicate blue and gold melodic phrases: ascending three-note curves, held horizontal notes, deliberate spaces, descending phrases and soft textured breath-like trails. Integrate a few tiny readable solfege syllables 'do', 're', 'mi' along an ascending phrase and 'mi', 're', 'do' along a descending phrase, as discreet handwritten score annotations rather than a headline or children's lesson. Show rising, holding, falling, pause, release. Refined editorial album-art sensibility, mature, minimal, lyrical and personal, with beautiful precise lines and subtle texture. Wide dark negative space in the upper third for website text. No guitar, no musical instruments, no singer portrait, no mouth, no microphone, no large symbols, no cartoon notes, no equalizer blocks, no neon, no futuristic interface, no logo or marketing slogan. Maintain enough visual contrast to read at thumbnail size. Decorative stock lines only, not an actual market record. The movement itself is the subject.
