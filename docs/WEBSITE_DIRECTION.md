supersedes the earlier cover-plus-journal direction. Preserve the player design.

- Render the supplied chronological runs without closing paths or joining gaps.
- Keep the fixed SG 11/cubic mapping and parent-range anchors in generation.
- Pitch determines line color. Brand artwork is only social-preview identity.
- Two synchronized voices: minute session movement and five-minute movement of

  This does not play five days of audio in the space of one day.
- Keep lowercase malosound and the exact phrase "price, played back.".
- Only essential playback, mute and date links. No public calendar, journal,
  trading outcomes, report pages, account data, events panels or marketing copy.
- No autoplay, invented observations, fake activity or manually composed daily
  soundtrack. The browser's initial instrument is deterministic additive synthesis.
- Public release is still on hold for Marcelo's review and data-use permission.
  Historical datasets must keep their actual dates; no automated-update promise.
